## The Business

**Northcote** is a SaaS tool for UK bridge lenders. Priced at **£350/seat/month**, it connects to Microsoft 365 (Outlook, OneDrive, Excel), reads every email and spreadsheet, links them to the correct loan deal, and surfaces overdue payments, approaching maturities, and unanswered emails. Every figure cites its source cell or email. Currently in private pilot with UK bridge lending operators.

## The Question

**Can Northcote reach £1M in revenue within 1.5–2 years?**

## 3. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | What It Does | Pricing | Northcote Differentiation |
|-----------|-------------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Risk Free (Impact)** | UK market leader. Loan servicing, origination, workflow, compliance. Used by Octopus, MT Finance, LendInvest | Enterprise (bespoke, est. £20K+/yr) | Traditional loan administration. Does NOT read emails or link communications to deals. Different entry point. |
| **LendFusion** | Specialist lending management — originate, service, scale. GDPR compliant. | From €1,659/mo (flat, unlimited users) | Modern but generic across lending types. No email intelligence. No source citation. |
| **BrightOffice** | UK-based SME lending management. Origination, underwriting, doc management. | From ~£500/mo | Broader SME focus, not bridge-specific. No AI layer. |
| **Kennek** | Newer entrant, modern LOS for bridging. Recently raised £20M. | Not public | Closest competitive threat — modern and bridge-focused, but loan origination-focused, not communications intelligence. |
| **The Mortgage Office** | Loan management for mortgages, bridging, P2P | Per-loan pricing | Legacy system. Servicing-focused, no communications intelligence. |

**Notable**: Competitor pricing is generally **flat-fee** (not per-seat), which means Northcote's per-seat model yields higher ACV at larger firms. LendFusion at €1,659/mo flat vs Northcote at £350 × 12 seats = £4,200/mo — Northcote is 2.5× the price but offers a fundamentally different capability (AI email + spreadsheet intelligence vs loan administration).

**Key insight**: Northcote is **not competing head-on** with loan servicing platforms. Its wedge is the **communications + data layer** — connecting emails and spreadsheets to deals with AI. This is a different entry point that existing platforms don't address. Over time, Northcote can expand into servicing; existing platforms cannot easily bolt on email intelligence.

## 4. Risk Analysis

| Risk | Severity | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|------------|
| **TAM ceiling** — only ~60–100 specialist bridge lenders in the UK | HIGH | High | Expand to development finance, commercial mortgages, specialist lending (adds 300–500+ firms). UK bridging alone caps at £3M–£6.3M ARR. |
| **Per-seat pricing resistance** — competitors charge flat fees; at 12+ seats Northcote costs 2–3× more | MEDIUM | Medium | Justify with ROI quantification (hours saved, payments caught). Offer volume discounts at 10+ seats. Consider hybrid pricing. |
| **Long sales cycles** — £50K ACV puts deals in mid-market territory (3–6 month cycles) | MEDIUM | High | 14-day trial removes risk. Target the ops lead, not the CTO. But expect 3–5 stakeholder buying group at this ACV. |
| **Competitive response** — Risk Free or LendFusion adds email AI | MEDIUM | Low (12–18mo) | Speed is the moat. Niche depth (bridge-specific entity recognition, deal linking) is hard to replicate generically. |
| **Data sensitivity** — lenders nervous about AI reading emails | MEDIUM | Medium | "Never used to train AI" commitment. Encryption at rest/transit. SOC 2 path. |
| **Onboarding bottleneck** — "one firm at a time" can't scale | HIGH | High | Must parallelise by month 6. Build self-serve onboarding or hire customer success. |
| **Churn** — small lenders churn when market softens | LOW | Low (market growing) | Annual contracts with monthly billing. Embed deeply into daily workflow. |

**Fatal vs manageable**: Only **TAM ceiling** and **onboarding bottleneck** are potentially fatal. Both have clear mitigations. All other risks are manageable with execution.

## 7. What Would Change This Assessment

### Kill signals (if observed, the £1M path is unlikely):
1. **Pilot conversion below 40%** — indicates product-market fit is weaker than the pilot suggests
2. **Average sales cycle exceeding 4 months** — the market buys slower than the model assumes
3. **Average seats per firm below 5** — ACV drops below £21K, needing 48+ firms which strains the narrow market
4. **A major competitor (Risk Free or LendFusion) ships email intelligence within 6 months** — the wedge closes

### Upgrade signals (if observed, £1M likely comes faster):
1. Pilot conversion above 80%
2. Inbound inquiries from firms not in the sales pipeline
3. Average seats per firm above 15
4. A broker proactively asks to refer clients

## Final Assessment

**Northcote is a viable startup with a realistic path to £1M ARR.** The problem is real, the product approach is right (read existing data, don't demand new workflows), and the market is booming at record levels.

The per-seat model is the key insight: at £350/seat with specialist bridge lenders averaging 10–20 employees, the ACV is ~£50K — meaning Northcote needs only **~20 paying firms** for £1M ARR. That is roughly 1 new customer per month after the pilot phase, in a market of 60–100 specialist firms. This is ambitious but tractable.

The single most important strategic lever is **sales velocity** — how fast Northcote can move from "one firm at a time" to parallel onboarding. The second is **market expansion** into development finance, which roughly doubles the addressable market and removes the TAM ceiling risk.

**The pricing risk cuts both ways**: per-seat at £350 yields higher ACV than flat-fee competitors (LendFusion at €1,659/mo flat vs Northcote at ~£4,200/mo for 12 seats). This is justified if the product delivers measurable ROI — but if firms push back on per-seat pricing, a hybrid model (base + per-seat) may be needed.

This is not a "swing for the fences" bet. It is a **grind-it-out vertical SaaS** in a well-defined niche with genuine pain, strong willingness to pay, and a growing market. The £1M target is realistic and the probability is higher than most pre-revenue startups — but it demands disciplined execution on the specific actions above.
