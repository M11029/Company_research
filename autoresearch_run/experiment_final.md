## The Business

**Northcote** is a SaaS tool for UK bridge lenders. Priced at **£350/seat/month**, it connects to Microsoft 365 (Outlook, OneDrive, Excel), reads every email and spreadsheet, links them to the correct loan deal, and surfaces overdue payments, approaching maturities, and unanswered emails. Every figure cites its source cell or email. Currently in private pilot with UK bridge lending operators.

## The Question

**Can Northcote reach £1M in revenue within 1.5–2 years?**

---

## 1. Market Size & Structure

The UK bridging loan market reached **£10.9B in 2024** (Mintel) and **£13B+ in 2025** (BDLA), with record completions of **£2.8B in Q1 2025** alone. Growth projection: **25% over five years** (~4.6% CAGR). Average loan size: **£540K** (Clifton PF, Q1 2025). 72% of brokers expect continued market growth.

## 3. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | What It Does | Pricing | Northcote Differentiation |
|-----------|-------------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Risk Free (Impact)** | Loan servicing, origination, workflow, compliance reporting | Enterprise (undisclosed, est. £20K+/yr) | Traditional loan administration system. Does NOT read emails or link communications to deals. Different entry point. |
| **LendFusion** | Lending management — originate, service, scale | From ~$500/mo | Modern but generic across lending types. No email intelligence. No source citation. |
| **BrightOffice** | SME lending management, UK-based | Mid-market pricing | Broader SME focus, not bridge-specific. No AI layer. |
| **The Mortgage Office** | Loan management for mortgages, bridging, P2P | Per-loan pricing | Legacy system. Servicing-focused, no communications intelligence. |

**Key insight**: Northcote is **not competing head-on** with loan servicing platforms. Its wedge is the **communications + data layer** — connecting emails and spreadsheets to deals with AI. This is a different entry point that existing platforms don't address. Over time, Northcote can expand into servicing; existing platforms cannot easily bolt on email intelligence.

## 4. Risk Analysis

| Risk | Severity | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|------------|
| **TAM ceiling** — fewer than 200 addressable firms in bridge lending alone | HIGH | Medium | Expand to development finance, commercial mortgages, specialist lending (adds 500+ firms) |
| **Low seat count** — micro lenders only need 2 seats, dragging ACV down | MEDIUM | High | Focus sales on small/mid segment (5+ seats). Introduce minimum deal at 3 seats. |
| **Long sales cycles** — financial services buying cycles of 3–6 months | MEDIUM | High | £350/seat is below most procurement thresholds. 14-day trial removes risk. Target the ops lead, not the CTO. |
| **Competitive response** — Risk Free or LendFusion adds email AI | MEDIUM | Low (12–18mo) | Speed is the moat. Niche depth (bridge-specific entity recognition, deal linking) is hard to replicate generically. |
| **Data sensitivity** — lenders nervous about AI reading emails | MEDIUM | Medium | "Never used to train AI" commitment. Encryption at rest/transit. SOC 2 path. |
| **Onboarding bottleneck** — "one firm at a time" can't scale | HIGH | High | Must parallelise by month 6. Build self-serve onboarding or hire customer success. |
| **Churn** — small lenders churn when market softens | LOW | Low (market growing) | Annual contracts with monthly billing. Embed deeply into daily workflow. |

**Fatal vs manageable**: Only **TAM ceiling** and **onboarding bottleneck** are potentially fatal. Both have clear mitigations. All other risks are manageable with execution.

## 5. Verdict: Most Likely Path to £1M

The most likely path to £1M ARR is **Scenario B+**:

- **Average 5 seats per firm** at £350/seat
- **Tiered minimum** introduced by month 6 (3-seat minimum)
- **40–55 firms** acquired by month 18–24
- **NRR of 110–115%** from seat expansion
- **Adjacent vertical** (development finance) entered by month 12

**This yields £850K–£1.2M ARR by month 18, and £1.3M–£2.0M ARR by month 24.**

### Conditions that must be true:
1. Pilot converts at ≥60% to paid
2. Average sales cycle stays under 3 months
3. Average seats per firm is ≥4
4. At least one broker channel partner by month 9
5. Onboarding parallelised to 3+ concurrent customers by month 6

**Probability of hitting £1M ARR within 24 months: ~55–65%**
**Probability of hitting £1M ARR within 18 months: ~30–40%**

## 7. What Would Change This Assessment

### Kill signals (if observed, the £1M path is unlikely):
1. **Pilot conversion below 40%** — indicates product-market fit is weaker than the pilot suggests
2. **Average sales cycle exceeding 4 months** — the market buys slower than the model assumes
3. **Average seats per firm below 3** — ACV too low, need 100+ firms which is unrealistic in 18 months
4. **A major competitor (Risk Free or LendFusion) ships email intelligence within 6 months** — the wedge closes

### Upgrade signals (if observed, £1M likely comes faster):
1. Pilot conversion above 80%
2. Inbound inquiries from firms not in the sales pipeline
3. Average seats per firm above 6
4. A broker proactively asks to refer clients

## Final Assessment

**Northcote is a viable startup.** The problem is real, the product approach is right (read existing data, don't demand new workflows), and the market is booming. At £350/seat with an average of 5 seats per firm, the path to £1M ARR requires ~48 firms — achievable within 24 months with focused execution, though tight at 18 months.

The single most important strategic lever is **sales velocity** — how fast Northcote can move from "one firm at a time" to parallel onboarding. The second is **market expansion** into development finance, which roughly doubles the addressable market.

This is not a "swing for the fences" bet. It is a **grind-it-out vertical SaaS** in a well-defined niche with genuine pain, strong willingness to pay, and a growing market. The £1M target is realistic but demands disciplined execution on the specific actions above.
