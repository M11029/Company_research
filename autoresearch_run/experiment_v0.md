# Northcote — Startup Viability Assessment v0
*Starting state — 2026-04-03*

---

## The Business

Northcote is a SaaS tool for UK bridge lenders priced at £350/seat/month. It connects to Microsoft 365 (Outlook, OneDrive, Excel), reads every email and spreadsheet, links them to the correct loan deal, and surfaces overdue payments, approaching maturities, and unanswered emails. Every figure cites its source cell or email.

Currently in private pilot with UK bridge lending operators.

## The Question

Can Northcote reach £1M in revenue within 1.5–2 years?

## Market Size

The UK bridging loan market reached ~£10.9B in 2024 and is projected to grow 25% over five years. There are an estimated 150–300+ specialist bridge lending firms in the UK. The market is at record volumes.

## Revenue Model

At £350/seat/month, revenue depends on seats per firm × number of firms.
- If a typical firm has 5 seats: £350 × 5 = £1,750/month per firm = £21,000/year
- Need ~48 firms for £1M ARR
- If a typical firm has 3 seats: £350 × 3 = £1,050/month per firm = £12,600/year
- Need ~80 firms for £1M ARR

## Assessment

The product solves a real problem. Bridge lenders do manage loans across Outlook and Excel. The per-seat pricing at £350/month is reasonable for financial services SaaS. The market is growing.

The main risk is that the addressable market may be small. With 150–300 firms and needing 48–80 of them, Northcote would need 25–50% market penetration in under 2 years, which is ambitious but not impossible for a well-targeted vertical SaaS.

## Verdict

Conditionally viable. The path to £1M exists but requires strong execution on sales.
