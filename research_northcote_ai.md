**Date**: 2026-04-03

## 1. Company Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Product** | Loan book management SaaS for UK bridge lenders |
| **Pricing** | £350/month (flat, no per-user fees) |
| **Stage** | Private pilot with UK bridge lending operators |
| **Integration** | Microsoft 365 (Outlook, OneDrive, Excel) |
| **Core value** | Connects emails, spreadsheets, and documents to deals with source citations |
| **Website** | northcoteai.com |

### What It Does
Northcote connects to a bridge lender's Microsoft 365 environment and:
1. **Reads every email and spreadsheet** — no templates or data entry required
2. **Links each item to the correct deal** — even when one email thread covers multiple loans
3. **Shows the source of every figure** — click any number to see the original cell or email

It surfaces overdue payments, approaching maturities, and unanswered emails proactively.

## 5. Scenario Analysis

### Scenario A: Current Trajectory (Base Case)
- Stays at £350/month, bridge lenders only
- Acquires 5–8 customers/month after pilot
- **18-month projection: 90–144 customers → £378K–£605K ARR**
- **Verdict: Misses £1M target**

### Scenario B: Price Expansion (Moderate Case)
- Introduces tiered pricing: £350 (small), £750 (mid), £1,500 (large)
- Blended ARPU rises to ~£700/month
- Acquires 4–6 customers/month
- **18-month projection: 72–108 customers → £605K–£907K ARR**
- **Verdict: Approaches but likely still short**

### Scenario C: Market Expansion (Bull Case)
- Expands to adjacent verticals: development finance, commercial mortgages, specialist lending
- Tiered pricing with blended ARPU of £800/month
- TAM grows to 1,000+ firms

## 7. Verdict

### Can Northcote reach £1M revenue in 1.5–2 years?

**It is possible but not on the current trajectory.**

**What's strong:**
- Exceptional problem–solution fit — this solves a genuine, painful problem
- The bridge lending market is booming (record volumes, 25% growth outlook)
- Low-friction integration (Microsoft 365 OAuth, no templates)
- £350/month is an easy "yes" budget decision for any lender
- Private pilot suggests real product with real users

**What must change to hit £1M:**
1. **Raise prices** — £350/month dramatically underprices the value for lenders managing £10M+ books. Tiered pricing (£500–£2,000/month based on loan book size) is the single highest-leverage move.
2. **Expand the market** — Bridge lending alone caps at ~£1.3M ARR even with 100% penetration. Development finance, commercial mortgages, and specialist lending are natural adjacencies.
3. **Accelerate onboarding** — "One firm at a time" won't reach 240 customers in 18 months. Needs to parallelise.

**Probability estimate:**
- At current pricing & scope: **15–20%** chance of hitting £1M in 2 years
- With pricing tiers + adjacent verticals: **45–55%** chance
- With all levers pulled (pricing + expansion + faster GTM): **60–70%** chance

### Final Assessment
Northcote is a **viable startup** with a sharp, well-defined wedge into a growing market. The product instincts are strong — reading existing data rather than demanding new workflows is the right approach. However, reaching £1M in 1.5–2 years requires deliberate strategic moves beyond the current pilot: higher prices, broader market, faster onboarding. The ceiling isn't the product — it's the current market scope and pricing.

**Rating: Conditionally Viable** — strong foundation, needs strategic expansion to hit the £1M target.
